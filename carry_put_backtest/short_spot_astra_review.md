# Independent review of the short carry-put and funded spot study

Review date: 2026-09-11. Scope: implementation, saved historical ledgers, sensitivities, and validation evidence. No strategy implementation or original historical artifact was changed during this review. No full historical repricing was rerun.

## Verdict

The inspected hedge signs, multiplier conversion, funded spot cash accounting, joint execution lag, and terminal liquidation are consistent with coding_plan.md. Independent reconstruction supports the reported baseline P&L and volatility. However, the validation/provenance layer needs correction before claiming that the historical results were generated by the exact current source code.

## Findings

1. **P1 — Historical execution hashes are overwritten with current source hashes.** `analyze_short_spot.py:209-223`, called at line 203, refreshes baseline/fine run manifests without executing the historical engine. This destroys evidence of which source generated a result. This is an actual discrepancy in this delivery: all 24 baseline checkpoints have engine.py, analyze_historical.py and analyze_short_spot.py hashes different from current files, while the aggregate baseline manifest matches current files for all three. Reporting.py matches all 24 checkpoints. Hash mismatch alone does not prove an economic change, but the refreshed manifest cannot establish reproducibility. Preserve execution manifests; write separate analysis manifests and explicitly report mismatches. Reconcile the source differences or rerun affected calculations after fixes, using matching calibration caches where valid.

2. **P2 — Test success is generated without test execution.** `analyze_short_spot.py:231-235` unconditionally writes status=passed and fixed counts of 7/78. This is not test evidence. Capture an actual subprocess exit code/output or consume an independently produced test result with its execution timestamp and source identity. Current independent test execution is reported separately below.

3. **P2 — Sensitivity results can silently come from an older baseline.** `analyze_short_spot.py:187-193` only checks for an existing cohort_pnl.csv before skipping replay. A new baseline, changed settings, or a partial prior sensitivity run can therefore leave stale results in the report. Key variants to baseline content and replay settings, or regenerate these cheap replays every time. In this delivery, fresh in-memory lag/cost replays match saved totals to 2.85e-14; no current numerical discrepancy was found.

4. **P2 — Historical completeness is not a validation gate.** `analyze_short_spot.py:122-131` checks present cohorts but never compares their IDs with all eligible coverage IDs. The runner intentionally persists partial batches, so an interrupted run can pass analysis and be reported as completed historical validation. Require exact eligible-ID equality for a full batch, plus uniqueness and consistency across daily/summary/audit files. Use an explicit pilot mode for subsets. The supplied baseline does contain all 24 eligible cohorts; this defect does not remove cohorts from the current result.

5. **P2 — Aggregate peak funding/exposure fields sum cohort peaks.** `reporting.py:34-37` applies sum to peak_borrowing and peak_spot_notional. For one futures plus spot, aggregate.csv consequently reports peak borrowing 66,807.67 and peak spot notional 67,372.36, although the largest individual active-cohort values are 4,669.81 and 4,713.35. Use max for maximum individual-cohort exposure, or reconstruct a dated funded portfolio to report a portfolio peak. A sum of separate peaks is neither. The dedicated exposure table uses max correctly.

Minor reporting issues: validation fields named factor/scale residual combine factor sensitivities with spot_delta_residual (scale divided by spot), so their units are mislabeled. Actual cash-scale residuals are nevertheless tiny: at most 4.55e-13 in both primary hedged scenarios. The baseline report uses a mean of cohort standard deviations, whereas the sensitivity table uses pooled daily standard deviation; both are labeled, but use one primary metric consistently. Seven new tests do not cover every acceptance case in the plan; in particular, add persistent unequal-multiplier/non-unit-quantity and rounded-futures tests. Independent review spot checks of those cases passed.

## Financial interpretation

All amounts below are points with the baseline unit multipliers. Daily risk is pooled discounted daily P&L standard deviation, excluding inception rows.

| Short one futures only | -1,285.77 | 29.312 | Negative |

Adding spot removes most of the large directional exposure introduced by the futures-only carry hedge. It reduces variance by about 98.2% versus the respective futures-only controls. Lower daily volatility does not imply higher realized profit or improved worst-case lifetime loss.

| Component | One futures + spot | Two futures + spot |
|---|---:|---:|
| Option premium less exercise payment | -80.45 | -80.45 |
| Futures P&L | -1,205.27 | -1,182.77 |
| Spot P&L | +1,035.03 | +1,016.22 |
| Financing | -25.92 | -25.49 |
| Total | -276.62 | -272.49 |

The futures-plus-spot trading legs lose 170.24/166.54 points before financing. Borrowing adds 25.92/25.49 of losses. These are not a double-counted spot loss: purchases are funded, spot price gains enter equity through the asset mark, and sale proceeds return to cash. The supplied paths therefore support the conclusion that the hedge reduces risk while having negative realized trading/funding contribution in this sample.

Only 3/24 one-futures-plus-spot cohorts and 4/24 two-futures-plus-spot cohorts are profitable, versus 10/24 unhedged. Worst cohort losses are 52.13/51.42 versus 48.77 unhedged. Thus lower pooled daily variance is not evidence that every tail or cohort improves.

Two futures improves aggregate P&L by only 4.13 points (0.17 per cohort) and daily standard deviation by about 0.55% relative to one futures plus spot. One futures already removes 99.9773% of the reported modeled carry-factor variance. The second instrument has little demonstrated incremental benefit in this sample; no statistically established superiority is claimed.

With 0.2 futures points and 1 bp spot costs, totals become -300.48/-296.12. Joint one-session lag gives -164.86/-162.17 but higher daily standard deviations 4.317/4.277; the better realized mean is not evidence that delayed hedging is systematically preferable.

The saved 2025-07-21 fine-grid pilot changes funded hedge P&L by +0.351/+0.353 points and retains the exercise outcome. This supports reporting rounded results, but one pilot does not establish historical convergence or the robustness of the small one-versus-two ranking.

Zero dividends on hypothetical tradable spot coexist with fitted observed futures carry. These assumptions need not define an arbitrage-consistent market. Historical OU parameters are provisionally used for pricing, entry premiums are model prices, execution is at the signal close in the baseline, and margin/liquidity/asymmetric funding are omitted. Residual P&L can reflect daily discretization, pricing/filter mismatch, exercise, and carry/financing assumptions. The decomposition alone cannot isolate their individual causal contributions or establish option mispricing. These are conditional hedging results, not an executable arbitrage or an annual portfolio return.
